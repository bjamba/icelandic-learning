1. Small Talk

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to: Start and maintain casual conversations with Icelanders.

Icelandic Small Talk

Icelanders enjoy casual conversations. Common topics:

  • Weather — Always a safe topic
  • Tourism — Many Icelanders work in tourism
  • Local events — Concerts, festivals
Pro tip: Icelanders appreciate honesty and sincerity. Don't force conversation if they seem busy!

Essential Phrases

Hvað ertu að gera?
What are you doing?
KVATH air-too aht GEH-ra?
Ég er að gera...
I am doing...
Ay-gh air aht GEH-ra
Hvernig hefur þú?)
How are you?
HVUR-veeth hay-vur THOO?
Ég hef það svona
I am doing okay
Ay-gh hayf THAHT SVOH-na
Þetta er gaman
This is fun
THET-a air GAH-man
Hvað ertu heimili?
Where are you from?
KVATH air-too HAY-mee-lee?
