2. Weather & Small Talk

The Icelandic Weather

Weather is a popular topic in Iceland. It changes quickly!

  • Sun, wind, rain, snow - all in one day
  • North Atlantic winds - always a topic
  • Midnight sun - summer phenomenon

Pro tip: Icelanders say "It is not bad weather, just wrong clothing!"

Essential Phrases

Hva veitir th?
What do you do?
KVATH WAY-teer THOO?
Thetta er gaman!
This is fun!
THET-a air GAH-man!
Hvaad er thad?
What is that?
KVATH air THAHT?
