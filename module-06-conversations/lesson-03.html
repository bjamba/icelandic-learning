3. Full Dialogues

Dialogue 1: Ordering at a Cafe

Server:
Godan dag, hva get eg geymt?
Good day, what can I serve you?
You:
Kaffi med mjolk, takk.
Coffee with milk, please.
Server:
Eitthvad ad borda?
Something to eat?
You:
Ja, eg vildi prufa kex.
Yes, I would like to try cookies.

Dialogue 2: Asking for Directions

You:
Kvehja, hvar er Kaennin?
Excuse me, where is the bank?
Local:
Faradu rett streti og svo vinstri.
Go straight and then left.
You:
Hvaad er thetta naalagt?
How far is it?
Local:
Thad er bara 5 minutur aadgangur.
It is just a 5-minute walk.
