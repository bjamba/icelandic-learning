2. At the Restaurant

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to: Navigate a restaurant menu, order food, ask for recommendations, and handle the bill.

Restaurant Etiquette

In Iceland, restaurants are generally casual. Here's what you need to know:

Service charge: Service is usually included in the price, so tipping is not expected (though appreciated for exceptional service).

Essential Phrases

Table for [number], please
