2. Public Transportation

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to: Use public buses in Reykjavik, understand routes, and buy tickets.

Buses in Reykjavik

Iceland's public transportation is reliable but limited. In Reykjavik:

  • Buses run from 6:30 AM to midnight
  • You need a Strætó card or cash
  • Route 1 goes to the airport and harbor
Tip: Download the Strætó app for real-time bus tracking and route planning.
