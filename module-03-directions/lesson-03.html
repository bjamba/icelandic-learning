3. Taxi & Transport

Taxis in Reykjavik

Taxis are available but expensive. Popular taxi companies:

  • Taxi: 555-5555
  • Strato Taxi: 550-5500
  • Black Taxi: 578-8888

Pro tip: Have your destination written down in Icelandic.
