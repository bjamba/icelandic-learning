2. Clothing & Souvenirs

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to: Buy clothing, find sizes, and shop for Icelandic souvenirs.

Icelandic Souvenirs

Popular Icelandic souvenirs include:

  • Lopapeyra — Icelandic sweater
  • Skógarmyndir — Nature postcards
  • Vínesja — Icelandic beer
  • Svið — Singed sheep head (for the brave!)
Pro tip: The Lopapeyra is the most popular souvenir. Look for the wool shop near Hallgrímskirkja!
