3. Store Emergencies

Returns & Exchanges

Iceland has good return policies. Key phrases:

  • Most stores accept returns within 14 days
  • Bring your receipt
  • Items must be unused

Pro tip: Keep your receipts in a digital photo album.

Essential Phrases

Thetta er vondur
This is broken
THET-a air VON-dur
Eg vilt retta
I want a refund
Ay-gh vilt RET-ta
Eg vilt skipta
I want to exchange
Ay-gh vilt SKIP-ta
Hvar er rettistofa?
Where is customer service?
HVAR air RET-tis-toh-fa?
