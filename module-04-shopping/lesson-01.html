1. Basic Shopping Phrases

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to: Ask about prices, sizes, and availability in Icelandic stores.

Shopping in Iceland

Iceland has good quality goods but prices can be high. Key phrases:

Pro tip: Many stores are closed on Sundays. Plan your shopping accordingly!

Essential Phrases

Hvað kostar þetta?
How much does this cost?
KVATH KOS-tar THET-a
Ég er að horfa á
I am looking at
Ay-gh air a DHOR-va
Hefur þú...?
Do you have...?
HEH-vur THOO
Ég vildi búa til mat
I would like to prepare food
Ay-gh VIL-tha BYOO-til mat
Þakka fyrir, ég er búin að fara
No thanks, I'm just looking
THAK-ka FVIR, ay-gh air BYE-in a THA-re
Ég er að leita að
I am looking for
Ay-gh air aLAY-ta
