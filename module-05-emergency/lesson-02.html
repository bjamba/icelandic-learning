2. Police & Safety

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to: Report incidents to police and understand safety instructions.

Police in Iceland

Iceland is one of the safest countries in the world. Police stations:

  • Reykjavik Police: 555 1111
  • Emergency: 112
Pro tip: Police in Iceland are very helpful and speak excellent English. Don't hesitate to ask for help!
