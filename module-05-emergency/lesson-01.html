1. Medical Emergencies

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to: Describe symptoms, call for medical help, and understand hospital instructions.

Emergency Numbers

  • 112 — Emergency (police, fire, medical)
  • 1770 — Health Center (Sjúkrahús)
  • 1700 — Poison Control
Important: In Iceland, most hospitals require payment upfront. Check if your travel insurance covers medical expenses!
