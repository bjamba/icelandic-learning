2. Introductions & Names

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to: Introduce yourself, ask someone's name, and respond to basic introduction questions.

Introducing Yourself

When meeting someone in Iceland, you'll want to introduce yourself properly. Here are the essential phrases:

Cultural note: Icelanders use first names in most situations, even in professional settings. The formal "þú" (you) is used in most contexts.

Essential Phrases

Ég heiti [nafn]
My name is [name]
Ay-gh HEH-tee
Hvað heitir þú?
What is your name?
KVATH HEH-teer THOO
Farðu vel
Nice to meet you
FAR-thoo VEL
Góðan dag, ég heiti [nafn]
Good day, my name is [name]
GO-than DIGH, ay-gh HEH-tee
Ég er frá [land]
I am from [country]
Ay-gh AIR fr-ah
Ég er bandaríkur
I am American
Ay-gh AIR ban-da-RIR-ook
