1. Hello & Basic Greetings

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to: Greet people formally and informally at any time of day, and understand common Icelandic greetings.

The Basics of Greeting

Icelandic greetings are straightforward but have formal and informal versions. The key things to know:

  • Formal: Use with strangers, elders, or in professional settings
  • Informal: Use with friends, peers, and children
  • Icelanders appreciate effort — even mispronunciation is welcomed
Pro tip: Smiling while speaking makes you sound friendlier. Icelanders may seem reserved at first, but they warm up quickly when you make the effort.
